# EMBRACE

**Disposition: promising social/motor hyperscanning resource; hold pending access/format audit.**

Sources: [Comani, Croce & Zappasodi, Zenodo, 29 October 2025](https://zenodo.org/records/17471747); [official EU project report](https://cordis.europa.eu/project/id/101007521/reporting).

| Item | Evidence / limitation |
|---|---|
| Cohort | 36 male dyadic recordings; some volunteers contribute to two dyads. **Do not infer 72 unique people.** Participant table maps people, dyads, dates and skill; exact distinct count/ages not yet audited. |
| Protocol/duration | Cooperative/competitive table tennis at easy/hard levels, randomized repetitions: 24 × 2.5 min dyadic bouts plus solo trials. Roughly 36 dyad-hours for these bouts, not 72 independent person-hours. |
| Signals/rates | Synchronized EEG, ECG, respiration, arm EMG and ball/body kinematics; native rates require sidecars. No gaze/EDA established. |
| Data/sync/standards | EEG SET/events; motion/EMG proprietary BTS TDF. BIDS 1.10-inspired organization explicitly **not fully compliant** because of TDF. Triggers distinguish conditions; residual precision NV. |
| Labels/repeats/artifacts | Cooperation/competition, difficulty, skill and task events; repeated sessions for some people. Strong exercise/movement artifacts and shared sensorimotor drive. Missing data/cap quality inventory NV. |
| Access/license/size/code | Zenodo metadata CC BY 4.0. Visible ZIP only 3,396,418 bytes, apparently repository/support material; full signal delivery and size not established. TDF reading may require proprietary tooling. No complete public preprocessing package verified. |

Supports joint-action physiology and multi-person/multi-system coordination, unusually rich in behavior and dyadic structure. Cannot infer empathy, social bonding or directed inter-brain communication merely from synchrony. Shared ball motion/task events are powerful alternative explanations.

At least the dataset record and official project report verified, not a full count of papers on the same cohort. Project already targets motion-artifact removal and multilevel synchrony analysis. Potential remains in stringent behavioral null models and robustness, but novelty/access are not certified. Evaluate connected components of the participant–dyad graph, not just dyad IDs, to prevent returning people appearing on both sides.
