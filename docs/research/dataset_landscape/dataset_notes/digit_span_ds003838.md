# Digit span / OpenNeuro ds003838

**Disposition: strongest general alternative; preferred if experimental control becomes the main priority.**

Sources: [Pavlov et al., Scientific Data 2022](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-022-01414-2); [OpenNeuro](https://openneuro.org/datasets/ds003838); [NEMAR mirror of OpenNeuro 1.0.6](https://www.nemar.org/dataset/on003838); [participant metadata](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds003838/master/participants.tsv). June 2026 NEMAR mirroring is not a new collection. The mutable metadata source was saved as an aggregate audit with a hash, not treated as a pinned release.

| Item | Evidence and qualification |
|---|---|
| Cohort | 86 healthy Russian-native participants. Direct metadata check: 74 female, 12 male; ages 18–44, mean 20.53 ± 3.90. Exclusion flags give 65 EEG, 83 ECG/PPG, 84 pupil and **64 participants with all four availability flags clear**, including behavior. This is metadata availability, not signal-quality certification. |
| Protocol/duration | Four-minute eyes-closed rest, then 162 auditory trials: 108 memory and 54 passive-listening controls; sequences of 5/9/13 digits every 2 s. Nine blocks, self-paced breaks. Full summed usable duration NV; do not multiply a guessed session length. |
| Signals/rates | 64-channel EEG plus ECG/PPG on the same ActiCHamp amplifier, 1000 Hz; Pupil Labs eye/pupil 120 Hz. No EDA/respiration. Native raw .set and pupil TSV, behavioral sheets and event codebooks; no resting pupil data. |
| Synchronization | EEG/cardiovascular share amplifier; event codes identify trial, item and recall. Eye-to-amplifier synchronization residuals/clock drift not established from inspected descriptor; verify before short-latency coupling. |
| Labels/controls/repeats | Item-level and serial-position recall, double-scored speech with adjudication; manipulated sequence length; NASA-TLX after every three blocks. Constant visual fixation/luminance supports pupil interpretation. All loads repeated within person; one visit, not longitudinal. Controls bracket memory blocks, so control versus memory also differs in block position. |
| Missingness/artifacts | Technical failures and misplaced caps. Paper's “3 + 19 EEG exclusions” does not arithmetically match 65/86; metadata contains **21 EEG exclusions**, confirming 65 but not resolving reasons. Blinks, oral recall and pulse quality matter. |
| Access/license/size | Open, CC0. NEMAR full representation reports 253.3 GB; EEG-only catalog sizes are not comparable. Full original native archive size not independently summed. |
| Documentation/standards/code | BIDS-organized, detailed events and participants metadata, PsychoPy stimulus script. Author reuse analyses have code; no single fully audited universal preprocessing pipeline. No local BIDS validation performed. |

Supports controlled memory-load/recall associations, individual differences, modality complementarity and physiological responses to successive items. Unusually valuable because objective behavioral responses and passive perceptual controls accompany simultaneous EEG, pupil and two cardiovascular measurements. Cannot equate prescribed digit count with actual experienced overload, establish clinical impairment, infer locus-coeruleus activity directly from pupil diameter, or demonstrate educational/real-world transfer. Heavy sex/age/language concentration, 64 metadata-complete participants and speech/motor phases limit generalization.

At least **the descriptor, three empirical journal analyses, one 2026 master's thesis and a 2026 software release** were verified as linked uses. [Kosachenko et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1111/psyp.14275) already tests EEG/pupil overload trajectories; [Pavlov et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-48917-5) examines PPG pulse amplitude, heart rate, pupil and performance; [Schmidt et al., eLife](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.100605) uses its ECG in a working-memory analysis of aperiodic activity. [UPM thesis, July 2026](https://oa.upm.es/97820/) already evaluates pupil–ECG load fusion. [2026 code release](https://zenodo.org/records/22109364) uses it for an independent heartbeat-locked EEG template; that is software/peer-review evidence, not a verified published clinical validation. Additional classifier papers were found but their accession/protocol correspondence needs checking.

Thus, “first multimodal load prediction” and discovery of inverted-U pupil/overload responses are unsafe novelty claims. Opportunities may remain in prediction of errors beyond nominal load, calibrated transfer, missing-modality robustness and disentangling shared artifacts from complementary physiology. A within-person repeated-trial design supports subject-held-out evaluation, but trials and windows do not replace independent people. Cross-modal ECG/PPG reconstruction is partly redundant measurement of the same cardiac source and needs an appropriate simple comparator.
