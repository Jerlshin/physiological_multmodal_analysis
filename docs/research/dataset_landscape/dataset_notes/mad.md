# MAD — Multimodal Affective Database (2026 preprint)

**Disposition: screened, not selected; public acquisition not verified.** Source: [author preprint, arXiv 2603.06206](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06206).

Reports 18 participants (9 men/9 women), ages 23–25, 16 clips over two days. Seventeen have complete physiological data. Signals include 14-channel EEG 128 Hz; ECG/EOG/EMG 1000 Hz, processed 256 Hz; PPG/BCG 34 Hz and three RGB-D views at 30 fps. Reported total size is about 8 TB, but stable public data download, file-level license and request success were not verified. Subject/day/clip identifiers and self-reported affect are described; no BIDS compliance established. Synchronization is described without an independently audited residual; missingness and artifacts require release inspection.

Selected 60 s peak segments across 18 × 16 trials amount to 4.8 h, not the whole 8 TB collection's duration. Peak selection can bias representativeness. Rich cameras/body signals are unusually useful for measurement comparisons, but tiny, narrow demographics and hardware/selection effects limit population conclusions. Supports affect and cross-view/physiological studies if released; cannot support generalized clinical or naturalistic emotion claims.

One preprint verified, already covering cross-subject EEG/domain adaptation, fusion, cardiac translation and multiple views. Therefore several obvious “new” tasks are occupied before release. Subject/day/clip holdouts and full unselected recordings would matter. No independent reuse verified; access uncertainty is not evidence that the dataset is underexplored in a scientifically useful way. Reconsider after a stable licensed release and a clear task-specific novelty gap.
