# Multimodal n-back with music

**Disposition: rejected as primary because released sample is much smaller than recruitment.** Source: [PhysioNet official v1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/multimodal-nback-music/1.0.0/), [data DOI](https://doi.org/10.13026/6vh4-dk68). Related paper predates the 2025 repository release.

Eleven recruited, but only **five subjects (3, 4, 6, 8, 11)** released because six lack usable facial measurements. Precise retained demographics NV. Two 964 s music phases give about 2.68 nominal participant-hours for these five, excluding other intervals. Includes processed 44-channel fNIRS at 7.6294 Hz, BIOPAC physiology acquired at 2000 Hz (ECG/EDA/PPG/respiration/temp/EMG), E4 signals and facial-analysis features. Facial features are not equivalent to raw video. Native complete waveform availability, precise synchronization residuals, license, bytes and complete executable processing package require archive inspection; no BIDS compliance established.

Task accuracy/RT and 1-/3-back condition offer objective behavior, but calming then exciting music is fixed-order. Subject/phase/block IDs support repeated-task analysis, not robust population evaluation. Supports exploratory workload/music associations and method demonstrations. Cannot establish music causality independently of order, generalizable multimodal learning or a high-capacity model's reliability at N=5. One resource/associated study identified; no independent reuse verified. Missing-modality research is conceptually interesting, but the unreleased missing cases cannot be analyzed as if available. Physiological breadth does not compensate for the effective cohort size.
