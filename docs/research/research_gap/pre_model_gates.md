## External validation: choose the scientific test, then the dataset

**Recommendation (I): plan later external validation.** BBBD can test bounded internal validity, but has too few independent content units and too much prior exploratory exposure to carry the intended broad claim alone. External validation is a design recommendation, not a formal journal rule.

| Route | What it could establish | Verified evidence / remaining uncertainty | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| John et al. 2025 educational gaze-cluster study (L07) | Cross-cohort replication; compare synchrony with heterogeneous attention allocation; pretest-adjusted immediate performance | Original paper inspected; 121 adults recruited, instructional-video gaze analyses include 105. Paper links [OSF 4rn5q](https://osf.io/4rn5q/). Access failed in this pass. Raw inventory/license, quiz joins, lineage and EEG availability for the **instructional** task are unverified; do not infer from EEG in the Hitchcock task | **First targeted access audit** for ocular/construct replication, not a verified tri-modal external dataset |
| Bühler et al. 2024 video-lecture cohort (L04) | Independent relationship among measured attention, gaze and learning; thought probes permit a construct contrast unavailable in BBBD | Original methods read: 84 quality-retained learners, one 60-minute statistics lecture, pre/post tests and probes. Public raw access/license and file compatibility not verified in this pass | Strong scientific lead; one lecture cannot validate broad content transfer |
| Other non-BBBD educational EEG/eye studies L01/L03/L08 | Replicate the precise behavioral endpoint with complementary measurement/pre-knowledge | Publication evidence varies; dataset accessibility and cohort independence must be checked. Shared authors/publications do not certify new participants; L01 may overlap historical BBBD lineage | Secondary lead queue, not certified validation resources |
| New prospective educational cohort | Confirm the hypothesis on untouched participants/content with known provenance; optionally measure gain/retention | Requires design, ethics, recruitment and calibrated acquisition; these actions are not started. Include more independent videos, pre-knowledge, documented quiz scoring, native triggers if temporal claims are intended, and planned missingness/quality information | Strongest route if accessible public cohorts do not match |
| ds003838 controlled auditory-memory resource | Test a general physiological-memory method with objective recall | Strong landscape alternative; [prior assessment](../dataset_landscape/selection_decision.md) documents metadata evidence. Different task, outcome and modality availability need explicit harmonization | Complementary mechanism/method validation, **not educational replication** |
| IMPROVE / affect / sleep datasets already reviewed | Sensor transfer, representation or missingness method components | Consumer EEG/HR are not necessarily EEG+ECG+eye; task semantics and access conditions differ. Restricted access is not acquired | Optional secondary method validation only |

Two independent samples with the same sensor but different targets are not equivalent to external validation of the same educational criterion. Conversely, an external gaze-only dataset can validate a bounded gaze result even if it cannot validate the tri-modal system. Report these scopes separately.

Do not concatenate unrelated cohorts simply to increase N. Keep domain-specific endpoints and evaluate transport with a prespecified mapping. Verify participant/source overlap with BBBD antecedents, permissions, sampling/scaling compatibility, stimulus identity and forecast chronology. Shared trained model weights may introduce an additional pretraining-overlap question.

## Scope after these gates

If the gates support G01, the next phase is a preregistered or time-stamped baseline/precision plan followed by bounded modeling, with external confirmation planned. Preregistration after this exploratory phase must disclose what has already been seen; internal holdouts are not retrospectively untouched hypothesis tests.

If physiology has no adequately precise incremental value, do not rescue the paper by changing targets to a high-accuracy attention-condition classifier. If provenance cannot support the intended measurements, switch to a defensible narrower sensor question or reconsider a landscape alternative. If the closest paper already answers the proposed question, reformulate before implementation. These are genuine stop/revise criteria, not an obligation to produce a positive model result.
