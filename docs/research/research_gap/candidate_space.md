## Proposed paper contributions and experimental stories

**G01.** A measurement-validity paper explaining when shared physiological responses can support individual quiz estimates, and when they cannot. First separate available covariates, quality and each sensor's contribution on identical cases; then compare known-content and novel-content access; finally reproduce the key boundary on an independent educational cohort. A method is warranted only if existing models systematically discard or confuse relevant information. A precise negative result can be valuable; an underpowered null cannot establish absence.

**G02.** A paper showing how eligibility and sensor availability alter the population for which a physiological learning measure is valid. Compare complete-case claims with an explicit available-sensor policy for all eligible outcome-observed participants; audit risk, coverage and uncertainty across quality strata; then stress-test observed versus assumed missingness mechanisms. Distinguish deployable observed-data risk from hypothetical fully measured risk throughout. Generic inverse weighting, gating and pattern calibration are established baselines.

**G03.** A paper identifying when expensive EEG measurement is useful during training but unnecessary at prediction. Establish teacher advantage first; compare direct ECG+eye learning, CCA, ordinary KD and existing physiological KD; then test whether any gain survives new learners/content and stimulus/quality-matched teacher controls. Measure retained behavioral utility and removal of sensors, not an unsupported claim of transferred brain mechanisms. Test another acquisition system before asserting practical accessibility.

**G04.** A paper mapping the limits of transporting a learning measure across exposure protocols for identical material. Fit within E4/E5 and transport in both directions on the three common tested videos, using training-only outcome baselines and score/quality sensitivity; require independent participant identity or downgrade the label of generalization. Treat E3 as a sparse additional stress test, not a fully crossed factorial design. Independent protocols and more shared content are required for a general transport claim.

**Recommendation (I):** prioritize G01 as the **primary question to validate next**. G02 and G06 are integral validity checks; G04 supplies a bounded stress test. G03 remains a separate, conditional extension. Do not combine them into an oversized architecture or promise four papers. The [primary formulation](primary_problem.md) is conditional on [pre-model gates](pre_model_gates.md), especially OQ-06, precision and the closest-paper review.
