## What exactly is the dataset, and why is it valuable?

BBBD combines five adult laboratory educational-video experiments:186 recruited,178 retained,11 unique videos, optical eye tracking throughout and electrical neural/cardiac/ocular recordings in experiments 2–5, with respiration in 4–5. Faster native signals are aligned and exported at 128 Hz. Quiz answers, selected prior-knowledge/trait measures and participant metadata allow physiology to be related to actual behavior. Its distinctive strength is simultaneous multimodal recording around shared naturalistic content, rather than a uniquely large or unstudied population. [S01–S06; INF-001]

## Strongest portions, portions to avoid, and promising modalities

- **Experiments 2–3:** strongest documented multimodal within-person attend/arithmetic contrast, with EEG/ECG/eye and quiz/trait information. Useful for replication and qualified protocol comparisons; fixed order prevents a pure attention-effect interpretation.
- **Experiment 4:** promising for a single-exposure immediate-quiz analysis using EEG/ECG/eye/respiration, provided corrected mappings and 16 Hz quality checks pass. Only the latter three videos have the described quiz linkage; N43 is small.
- **Experiment 5:** useful for studying the bundled repeat/preview/incentive setting and transport sensitivity; avoid treating it as an isolated randomized reward effect or pooling its final quiz uncritically with first-exposure outcomes.
- **Experiment 1:** useful eye-only comparison and historical replication; cannot support EEG/ECG fusion. Its phenotype-reference mismatch needs resolution.
- **EEG + ECG + eye signals** offer the clearest complementary measurement opportunity. ECG is already established in prior work; the untested question is incremental robust information, not whether ECG was ever used. Respiration is a narrower, smaller-cohort addition and had null synchrony/quiz findings in P03; a null in that analysis does not make it scientifically useless. EOG/head are valuable nuisance/measurement channels as well as possible predictors, but their semantics must be respected. [P02–P04; INF-025]

Avoid old/mixed-version labels, unmasked heavily interpolated segments for timing claims, physiological interpretations of 16 Hz electrical contamination, anonymous window-level random splits, and feature names whose computation has not been verified. Do not avoid entire cohorts merely because a risk exists: establish an evidence-based inclusion rule first. [Quality assessment](data_quality.md)

## Defensible targets and already-published work

Defensible outcomes are immediate quiz performance with correct item scoring/exposure alignment, narrowly named experimental-condition discrimination, and explicitly defined physiological coupling/traits. Long-term retention, ADHD diagnosis, natural mind-wandering detection and isolated causal reward effects are not established labels. [Labels/tasks](labels_and_tasks.md)

Four confirmed antecedent empirical papers already report: eye synchrony with quizzes (PNAS 2021); cardiac synchrony under distraction (Cell Reports 2021); multimodal synchrony/PCA, temporal analysis and held-out linear brain–body prediction (PNAS Nexus 2022); and EEG synchrony with WMC/GPA/ASRS and quiz models (iScience2025). The descriptor adds technical validation. A2026 IEEE classifier paper is a material unresolved lead. Some person/video holdouts already exist, but they do not establish a strict joint unseen-person/unseen-video quiz forecast. [Prior-work matrix](prior_work_matrix.md)

## Evidence-backed candidate directions for RESEARCH GAP & PROBLEM FORMULATION

These are **OUR INFERENCES / hypotheses**, not chosen problems or claims of first use. Prioritize by scientific question and audit feasibility, not architecture preference.

## Methodological mistakes future work must avoid

Treating condition instructions or ISC as independent attention truth; claiming ECG/fusion/temporal modeling is new; conflating observed association with predictive error; adding reused cohort Ns; leakage through group references or preprocessing; ignoring shared videos and repeated exposures; using partial-credit scores as ordinary binomial counts without checking; claiming native acquisition fidelity from a 128 Hz raw folder; trusting feature names over computations; and interpreting null trait coefficients as evidence of absence. [Prior-work dossiers](prior_work.md); [quality assessment](data_quality.md)

## What actual downloaded-data inspection must resolve

First verify 1.1 bytes and subject/quiz joins, then canonical video/session identities, scoring rules, per-modality usable intersections, interpolation/missingness masks,16 Hz contamination, scaling/reference/padding, and synchronization residuals. Reconcile the public RMSSD calculation and historical label mappings before using descriptor results as benchmarks. Retrieve inaccessible supplements/P05 and inspect pretrained-data manifests before novelty claims. The [27-item audit queue](open_questions.md) gives evidence needed to close each issue.

**Stage boundary:** no final direction is selected. The permanent evidence base supports the next stage of research-gap and problem formulation after these targeted gates are resolved.

## Empirical audit amendment — 2026-10-02

The historical assessment above is retained. The [actual-file report](../dataset_exploration/README.md) substantially resolves acquisition, release-key joins/scoring, coverage and lightweight QC. All five canonical archives match official sizes/MD5; 16,038 continuous files and 180,221 channel records were audited with no parser failures. There are 178 experiment-qualified participant records, 11 URL-identified videos, 1,474 design-expected exposures (1,457 observed), and 736 unambiguous phenotype memory outcomes. Cross-experiment unique-human identity remains unverified.

The EEG+ECG+eye subset with direct quiz linkage after provisional QC is **120 qualified people / 444 person-video observations / 11 videos**, not the full nominal cohort. Per experiment: 2 = 26/120/5; 3 = 24/124/6; 4 = 33/92/3; 5 = 37/108/3. Adding respiration yields 70/200/3. Valid durations are exact released-grid mask unions; they do not certify native clock accuracy. [Exact intersections](../dataset_exploration/modality_coverage.md)

The [empirical feasibility matrix](../dataset_exploration/research_feasibility.md) assigns conditional MEDIUM to C01–C04, LOW to the currently specified C05/C06, and HIGH to demonstrable release-integrity/measurement auditing within C07. Exact historical-paper reproduction remains conditional on unavailable original inputs. These judgments do not choose a question or establish sufficient statistical power. Eye correction spans can last minutes; 16 Hz peaks are localized and retained as flags; the supplied HRV notebook expression is RMS RR rather than successive-RR RMSSD. [QC](../dataset_exploration/signal_quality.md); [HRV](../dataset_exploration/ecg_quality.md)
