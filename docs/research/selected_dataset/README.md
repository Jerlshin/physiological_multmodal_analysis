**Evidence language.** VERIFIED FACT means directly observed in the cited documentation/code or an explicitly reported study result; it does not mean independently reproduced. AUTHOR-REPORTED LIMITATION identifies the authors' own caveat. OUR INFERENCE is a reasoned interpretation. OPEN QUESTION is unresolved. Repository reference assets, historical publication cohorts and corrected release files are distinct evidence objects.

**Scope of completion.** Original full texts of the descriptor and four confirmed antecedent empirical papers were read, alongside official web documentation, schema assets and a pinned code snapshot. Supplements were located, but three separate supplementary PDFs could not be retrieved; their contents are not claimed verified. A 2026 IEEE classifier paper remains a methods-access and dataset-lineage lead. The 27 GB waveform release was not downloaded or tested. Consequently this is a documentation/code evidence review, not a completed waveform, timing or numerical replication audit. Negative literature findings are bounded search results, not proofs of absence.

Verification results are recorded in [review_validation.json](evidence/review_validation.json): all required files, local links and inference references checked; aggregate reference-table audit reproduced; figures inspected. These checks validate the review artifacts, not the scientific results.
