**Zero coding finding (IS-003).**

- **No literal zeros.** Processed EEG never contains an exact physical 0. The 16-bit release cannot represent 0 µV.
- **Zeroed runs exist.** Author-zeroed artifact runs are present as the bracketing digital codes, with runs up to 195 samples in the survey.
- **Consequence.** Q "EEG zeroed fraction" and the RF/SYN validity rules are therefore applied in the digital domain. In PAS-core the zeroed fraction has median 0.0002, 95th percentile 0.011 and maximum 0.21.

## Pre-outcome observations (recorded before any outcome access; inference register BII-004…BII-007)

1. **EEG ISC shares variance with gaze behaviour.**
   - Spearman ρ(SYN1, RF5 gaze dispersion) = 0.48 and ρ(SYN1, RF6 saccade rate) = 0.37.
   - Raw-route SYN1 averages 0.40, against 0.23 in the processed route. The raw route keeps more stimulus-locked ocular activity than the authors' RPCA/interpolation pipeline.
   - An H2b (EEG) result therefore cannot by itself be read as non-ocular neural information.
2. **Route agreement** (Spearman, PAS-core, processed versus raw):

   | Feature | ρ |
   |---|---|
   | RF1 alpha | **0.60** |
   | RF2 theta | 0.81 |
   | SYN1 | 0.80 |
   | Gaze RF/SYN | ≥ 0.999 |
   | ECG (route-independent) | 1.00 |

   The raw-route sensitivity (H3.4) is a materially different EEG measurement, which is the reason it was prespecified.
3. **Quality extremes.** One PAS-core/PAS-four cell has an EEG 16 Hz excess of 7–8 dB.
   - In a synthetic-null smoke run, a baseline (M2) fold with little shrinkage extrapolated its linear Q effect to that cell. The M5−M2 point estimate rose to 0.095, with six cells carrying 95% of it.
   - This is a property of the frozen design (separately tuned single-penalty ridge, linear Q), not a leak: the outcomes were synthetic and independent of every feature.
   - The permutation test absorbs it (V2). The prespecified matched-noise control (M2+noise vs M2) quantifies it and must be read next to H1.
4. **Physiology–quality coupling** (|ρ| > 0.3 among RF, SYN and Q):
   - gaze versus pupil correction (0.72);
   - HR versus ln RMSSD (−0.68);
   - SYN1 versus SYN2 (0.41);
   - RF5 versus SYN3 (−0.38).

   The Freedman–Lane residualization on C and Q is designed for this collinearity. V2 checks its calibration on these real features.
